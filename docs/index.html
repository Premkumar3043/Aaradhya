A deeper destination story

Kashi, where the day begins at the river.

Varanasi rises along the western bank of the Ganga. Pilgrims arrive for darshan at Kashi Vishwanath, one of the twelve Jyotirlingas, and stay for the ghats, where mornings begin with boat rides and evenings close with the Ganga aarti.

The old city is a maze of narrow lanes, so the best plans keep a slow pace, a trusted guide on request and clear notes on timings.

Deity
Lord Vishwanath
Best season
October to March
Suggested stay
2 to 4 days
