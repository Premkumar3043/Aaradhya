## Notice

Most of the photographs are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA). If you redistribute a modified version of those photographs, follow the applicable share-alike terms. The Meenakshi photograph is CC BY 4.0. For every file, follow the full licence text and attribution instructions on its Commons page. These images are remotely loaded from Wikimedia Commons so that the ZIP remains lightweight; an internet connection is required to display the photographs. If a photograph does not load, the original vector temple illustration remains as a visual fallback.
